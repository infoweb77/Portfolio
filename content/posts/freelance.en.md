### Dr.NearMe App

An app for searching for doctors/patients, booking appointments/managing appointment logs, and organizing contacts.

I was responsible for map functionality and display. Started with native Apple MapKit, later the team decided to migrate to Google Maps. Integrated a clustering library.

A specific challenge involved displaying filters on the map based on doctor availability (available appointment times / N/A if unavailable), specialty, or doctor avatar.

I was also responsible for the layout of screens displaying lists of doctor cards.
